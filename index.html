Fintech · UX Research + Product Design

PayNora

A payments app built for senior and cautious users - the people most likely to fear a wrong tap. Guided flows, voice-assisted confirmation, and a risk-free practice mode let them build real confidence before real money moves.

  • Addressed fraud anxiety and fear of mistakes head-on
  • Simplified navigation for low digital-confidence users
  • Practice-based learning before live transactions

EdTech · UX Research + Visual Design

Headroom

A study companion for students preparing for competitive exams - designed around burnout, not around streaks. Soft colour, rounded components, and gentle feedback keep the pressure down while the momentum stays up.

  • Designed for emotional pressure, not just productivity
  • A calm visual system: soft colour, rounded forms, clear hierarchy
  • Gentle feedback loops that support recovery, not guilt
